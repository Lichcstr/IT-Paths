ABOUT - IT PATHS

What is IT Paths?

IT Paths is an educational website designed to help students explore different career opportunities in Information Technology. It provides information about various IT professions, helping students understand their roles, required skills, and possible career paths.

Who Is It For?

IT Paths is made for students, beginners, and anyone interested in pursuing a career in Information Technology, especially those who are unsure which field to choose.

What Is It Used For?

The website serves as a career exploration guide where visitors can discover IT professions, learn about their responsibilities, and identify career options that match their interests and skills.
